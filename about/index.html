About encodeb64.com

What is encodeb64.com?

encodeb64.com is a free online tool for encoding text, files, and images to Base64 directly in your browser. It supports UTF-8 encoding with correct non-ASCII handling, Base64URL (RFC 4648), MIME line splitting (76-char chunks per RFC 2045), per-line encoding, file encoding up to 100MB, and an Image to Base64 converter supporting JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, SVG, BMP, ICO, TIFF, and AVIF.

Who built this?

encodeb64.com was designed and developed by MpmDigital, a digital agency specialising in web development and online tools.

Our philosophy

We believe developer tools should be fast, private, and free. All processing on encodeb64.com happens entirely in your browser — no data is ever sent to a server. No registration required, no usage limits, no ads injected into your encoded content.

Sister tool

encodeb64.com is the encoding companion to decodeb64.com, which handles Base64 decoding of text, files, and images.
